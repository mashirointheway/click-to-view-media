Chrome · Firefox for Android

Blurred until you click.

Images and video stay covered until you want them. Click one to see it, click again to hide it.

Download How it works

Chrome installs from a folder. Firefox on Android installs over USB.

How it works

  1. Page loads

    Pictures and video are covered.

  2. Click one

    Only that item clears.

  3. Click again

    It goes back to blurred.

Features

  • Everything visual

    Photos, video, background pictures, emotes, embedded players.

  • New content

    Media added as you scroll is covered as it arrives.

  • Two settings

    Blur from 18 to 100 pixels, and a switch for the current site.

  • Stays on your device

    No account, no network requests, nothing collected.

YouTube ads

YouTube ads are muted while they play, Skip is clicked, and your volume comes back after. Depends on YouTube's player.

Install

Chrome

manual install, about two minutes

  1. Download and unzip.
  2. Open chrome://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode.
  4. Load unpacked, then pick the folder.
